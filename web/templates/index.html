Stage 3.1-3.2 • Variational Compression (VAE)

Current quantum hardware supports 8–32 high-fidelity qubits. Rather than linear PCA, a Variational Autoencoder (VAE) maps 768 dimensions into a Gaussian latent distribution ($\mu, \sigma^2$) using the reparameterization trick $z = \mu + \sigma \odot \epsilon$, discarding stochastic noise while preserving non-linear clinical geometry.

Stage 3.3-3.4 • Adaptive Selection & Contrastive Learning

Learned feature gates adaptively choose different biomarker subsets depending on patient pathology (e.g. Vessel tortuosity & HbA1c for ophthalmic cases vs Troponin-I & ST-elevation for cardiac cases). Supervised contrastive learning clusters patients with similar disease phenotypes closer together in Hilbert space.

Stage 3.5-3.7 • Quantum Readiness & 16-D Latent Vector

Applies L1 sparsity constraints to eliminate redundant channels, followed by $L_2$ unit-sphere normalization ($\sum |x_i|^2 = 1$). Produces the final 16-Dimensional Quantum-Ready Latent Vector ready for direct state preparation across 4 qubits.

Mathematical & Quantum Circuit Formulations

Formal mathematical grounding of quantum feature mappings, parameterized circuits, and error mitigation

1. 4-Qubit Amplitude Encoding Formulation
|\psi(x)\rangle = \sum_{i=0}^{2^n - 1} x_i |i\rangle, \quad \text{where } \sum_{i=0}^{15} |x_i|^2 = 1, \; n = 4 \text{ qubits}

Maps 16 continuous normalized biomedical latent features into the probability amplitudes of 4 entangled qubits, enabling exponential compression of high-dimensional multi-modal medical signals.

2. ZZ-Feature Map Entangling Unitary
U_{\Phi(x)} = \exp\left(i \sum_{j=1}^4 \phi_j(x) Z_j + i \sum_{j < k} \phi_{jk}(x) Z_j Z_k\right), \quad \phi_{jk}(x) = (\pi - x_j)(\pi - x_k)

Creates quantum entanglement between paired physiological biomarkers (e.g. blood pressure and retinal caliber narrowing), mapping them into a high-dimensional Hilbert space.

3. Quantum Kernel Inner Product Estimation (QSVM)
K(x, x') = |\langle \psi(x) | \psi(x') \rangle|^2 = |\langle 0^{\otimes n} | U_{\Phi(x)}^\dagger U_{\Phi(x')} | 0^{\otimes n} \rangle|^2

Directly evaluates quantum fidelity between two patient states on quantum hardware, providing superior decision margins on complex somatic gene mutation distributions.

4. Zero-Noise Extrapolation (ZNE) Richardson Error Mitigation
\langle O \rangle_0 = \sum_{j=1}^m \gamma_j \langle O(\lambda_j) \rangle, \quad \text{such that } \sum \gamma_j = 1, \; \sum \gamma_j \lambda_j^k = 0

Scales hardware noise factors $\lambda \in \{1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5\}$ via unitary gate folding, fitting a polynomial to extrapolate expectation values to the theoretical zero-noise limit ($\lambda \to 0$).
